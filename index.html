About

What JEE Prep actually is

A free, no-clutter practice site built chapter by chapter for JEE Main Physics. Every question comes with a written reason for each wrong option, not just the right one — so a wrong guess still teaches you something. Built and maintained by Urani Software.

Instructions

How to use the practice app

Five steps, start to finish.

1

Create a free account

Sign up with your email from the "Log in" button in the top right. No payment, no card details.

2

Pick your chapter

Use the subject dropdown to choose a Physics chapter. Unit 1 (Units and Measurements) is fully loaded with questions today; more chapters are being added.

3

Choose a category

Start with Key Concept Questions if you're short on time, or go straight to Real JEE Questions if you want full exam difficulty.

4

Answer, section by section

Each chapter is split into sections that unlock in order — finish one to open the next, so you build up rather than skip around.

5

Read the explanation, then move on

Whether you got it right or wrong, read why the other options don't work — that's where most of the learning happens. Switch on Test Mode in the sidebar if you want feedback held back until you submit the whole section, like the real exam.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is JEE Prep really free?

Yes. Every chapter, question, and category currently available is free to use.

Do the questions match the real JEE Main syllabus?

Yes — the Syllabus tab inside the app lists the full official JEE Main Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics syllabus, and questions are written to match it chapter by chapter.

Can I retake a section after finishing it?

Yes. Sections only lock going forward — once you've completed one, you can reopen and retake it any time.
